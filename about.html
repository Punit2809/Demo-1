Our Story

Fifteen Years of Connecting Talent to Opportunity

Nexora began in 2011 with a simple observation: technology was moving faster than hiring could keep up. Founded by engineers and recruiters who had lived both sides of the interview table, we set out to build a staffing firm that actually understood the work it was hiring for.

Today, we're a global team of 120+ specialists across four continents, placing thousands of candidates a year in everything from seed-stage startups to the Fortune 500. What hasn't changed is our core belief — that the right hire changes everything.
