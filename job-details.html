About the Role

CloudForge is looking for a Senior Full-Stack Engineer to help build the next generation of our cloud-native delivery platform. You'll own features end-to-end — from React front-ends to scalable Node.js services on AWS — and mentor a small team of mid-level engineers along the way.

This is a high-impact role on a fast-moving product team. You'll collaborate daily with product, design and platform engineering to ship features used by thousands of developers every day.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build and ship full-stack features across React and Node.js.
  • Architect scalable, well-tested APIs and services on AWS.
  • Collaborate with product and design to translate requirements into technical plans.
  • Write clean, maintainable code and champion code reviews.
  • Mentor and support mid-level engineers on the team.
  • Contribute to CI/CD, observability and reliability improvements.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience.
  • Strong proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript, React and Node.js.
  • Hands-on experience with AWS services (Lambda, DynamoDB, S3, ECS).
  • Solid understanding of REST APIs, testing and system design.
  • Experience with relational and NoSQL databases.
  • Excellent communication and a collaborative mindset.

Benefits

  • Competitive salary plus equity package.
  • 100% remote with flexible working hours.
  • Comprehensive health, dental and vision coverage.
  • Annual learning & development budget.
  • Home-office stipend and latest hardware.
  • Generous parental leave and unlimited PTO.
